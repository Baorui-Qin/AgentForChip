# Agent Note: P10 bounded recovery and best observed revision

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-09-21-p10-repair-v2.zh.md)

## Problem

Reasoning-only truncation can consume a repair opportunity without a tool action. Severity-only parsing discards useful XSim hints. Continuing from a worse candidate compounds regressions, while missing evidence cannot safely be ranked as design failure.

## Decision

The [P10 repair protocol](../../../../rtl-tools/phase1/p10/README.md#repair-v2) separates normal and action-only output budgets, compacts completed history with a deterministic factual core, preserves bounded XSim and VCD observations, and restores the best evidence-bound revision before further repair. All model-visible inputs are persisted before dispatch. No shared provider or released session schema changes are required.

This extends, rather than replaces, [repair reachability](../bug-fix/2026-09-20-p10-repair-reachability.md) and [transport evidence](../bug-fix/2026-09-21-p10-transport-evidence.md). Their same-wire retry accounting, immutable historical campaigns and accepted-edit verification requirements remain active. Changed repair prompts and budgets require a new campaign freeze; historical results do not acquire the new policy retroactively.

## Alternatives considered

**Only enlarge the token limit.** More output capacity alone does not enforce an action. The bounded recovery request disables reasoning and rejects a response without exactly one allowed complete tool call. The existing provider interface cannot force tool choice or implement a mid-stream reasoning-token deadline.

**Summarize evidence with another model.** This introduces cost and unsupported interpretations. Deterministic compaction retains source, binding, observed failure and rejected revisions without turning model hypotheses into evidence.

**Rank every failure by mismatch ratio.** Compilation, missing evidence and unequal sample counts are not comparable ratios. Stage ordering applies only to complete design evidence; incomparable results restore the incumbent and stop.

**Parse private WDB or infer sampled values from VCD.** Existing XSim VCD and raw hints are sufficient for bounded timestamp windows. Timestamp-end values do not establish testbench sampling values, cycles or root causes, so those claims remain absent.

## Consequences

The search intentionally cannot cross an equal-or-worse intermediate revision. It preserves rejected sources and results for inspection, and only a fully verified current source can be exported as PASS. Model, active-time and tool-step limits remain effective after compaction and recovery. Missing optional waveforms do not invalidate independent verdict evidence.

## Testing

Focused tests exercise output recovery budgets, reasoning-off wire fields, invalid/partial action rejection, context overflow with retained bindings, regression restoration, rejected-source reuse and bounded waveform observations. A model-visible feedback snapshot records the deterministic projection. Historical Prob078 and Prob104 evidence is replayed without changing its files; real-provider repair success and new Vivado runs require a separately frozen campaign and are not established by these tests.
