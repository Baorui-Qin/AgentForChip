## Analysis

Let me trace the FSM behavior against the specification.

**Spec:** After entering B, sample `w` over 3 consecutive cycles; set `z=1` in the following cycle iff exactly two of the three samples were 1. Then repeat.

**Correct transition logic (counting ones after each sample):**
- After sample 1 (`B`): count 0→`C0`, 1→`C1` ✓ (matches current)
- After sample 2 (`C0`/`C1`): 0→`D0`, 1→`D1`, 2→`D2` ✓ (matches current)
- After sample 3 (`D0`/`D1`/`D2`), final count and `z`:
  - `D0` (count=0): final = 0 or 1 → **never 2 → `E0` unconditionally**
  - `D1` (count=1): final = 1 or 2 → `E1` iff `w=1` ✓
  - `D2` (count=2): final = 2 or 3 → `E1` iff `w=0` ✓

The existing `D0: next_state = w ? E1 : E0;` is a **defect**: with two zeros already seen, a third `w=1` yields a total of one, so `z` must stay 0. This wrongly asserts `z=1` for groups whose first two samples are `0,0` and third is `1`, matching the ~1/8-per-group mismatch rate observed.
